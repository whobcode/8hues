# 8hues

**because you got options…**

### [Open 8hues →](https://8hues.com)

8hues is a map-based social community for adult (18+) gay men. It's a place to
see who's around, talk, and decide for yourself how visible you want to be.

Free to join. Free to use. No app store required — it opens in your browser and
installs to your home screen if you want it there.

---

## What you get

**A live map.** See who's nearby, right now. Tap anyone to open their profile,
send a message, or star them for later.

**Privacy you set yourself.** Your exact location is never what other people
see. You choose how big an area you could be anywhere in, and you can go
incognito whenever you want to look without being seen.

**Move your pin, free.** Travel mode lets you type any address and browse that
area as if you were there. Planning a trip, or just curious what's happening
across town — it costs nothing.

**Eight colour schemes.** Pick the one that suits you. The whole app changes
with it, and each colour changes something real about how you show up.

**Real people only.** There are no filler profiles, no seeded accounts, and no
bots padding out the map. If you see someone, they're a person who signed up.

**Talk your way.** One-to-one messages, a public chat, and video calls — just
the two of you, or a group — when you'd rather see who you're talking to.

**Sign in without a password.** Use Face ID or Touch ID, or have a sign-in link
emailed to you. A password works too, if you prefer one.

## Free and Plus

Everything core to 8hues is free — the map, your profile, messages, photos,
travel mode, and all eight colour schemes.

**8hues Plus** adds:

- Incognito browsing
- Read receipts on your messages
- See who viewed your profile
- Video rooms
- No ads

Plus is a month at a time, and there's no subscription to cancel — it simply
ends unless you buy another. **New Plus purchases are paused at the moment.**
Anything you already have keeps working until the day it runs out, and you can
leave your address in the app to be told when Plus is back.

## Your privacy

- **Where you are.** Others see you somewhere inside a circle you size
  yourself, never your exact spot, and never closer than half the distance you
  choose. Your own pin is the only true one, and only you see it.
- **Safety mode** (Settings → Location privacy) goes further: you only ever
  show up somewhere within about 13 miles, your dot stays put while you move
  around the same area, nobody sees how far away you are, guests can't see you,
  and notifications don't say who or what. It turns on by itself in countries
  where being gay is against the law.
- **Photos** are visible only to signed-in members. Hidden location and camera
  details are stripped from every photo. Photos you send in a private message
  can be opened only by the two of you. Remove a photo and it's deleted from
  our servers, not just hidden.
- **Your data.** We don't sell it, and we don't share it for advertising. The
  [privacy policy](https://8hues.com/legal#privacy) names every service that
  helps run 8hues and what each one sees.
- **Deleting your account.** Email [privacy@8hues.com](mailto:privacy@8hues.com)
  from the address on your account. You can ask for a copy of your data the
  same way.

## Safety

8hues is 18+, and asks everyone for a date of birth. It isn't shown to anyone.
Members are not identity-verified, and 8hues doesn't run criminal background
checks, so treat anyone new the way you'd treat anyone you met anywhere else:
meet in public first, tell someone where you're going, and trust your read on a
situation.

- **Block or mute anyone.** Blocking is mutual and immediate: they disappear
  from your map, your messages, and your profile, and you disappear from
  theirs.
- **Report** with the 🚩 button and say what happened. A person reads every
  report, and reports of someone under 18, or of an intimate image shared
  without consent, reach a person straight away. Any photo opened full screen
  has **Report this photo**.
- **An intimate image of you shared without your consent?**
  [8hues.com/takedown](https://8hues.com/takedown) gets it removed within 48
  hours, with every copy, even if you don't have an account. You can also email
  [takedown@8hues.com](mailto:takedown@8hues.com).
- **The [safety center](https://8hues.com/safety)** has tips for meeting
  people, how reports are handled, and where to get help.

8hues isn't available in countries where being gay can carry the death penalty,
because an app that shows who is nearby could put people there in danger.
