# What's new on 8hues

Every change members can see, newest first. Dates are when it went live.

---

## August 2026

**22 August — Faster start.** The app no longer loads the map before you've
signed in, so the sign-in screen appears sooner, especially on mobile data.

**22 August — Video rooms know who you are.** Calls now carry your profile
identity, so people see your name rather than a code.

**21 August — 200 memberships to give away.** Invite links now come with Plus
attached, so the people you bring in start with everything switched on.

**19 August — Invites.** Share a personal link to bring someone straight to
8hues.

**18 August — Places, and only real people.** Local places moved into their own
panel, and members can add the ones they know. At the same time every filler
profile was removed from the map — everyone you see is a real person who signed
up.

**17 August — Real places in every city.** All 73 city pages now list actual
local places rather than generic suggestions.

**16 August — Email that arrives.** Sign-in links, verification and password
resets now reach you reliably, including on Apple and Gmail addresses.

**16 August — Plus, and a free Halloween.** 8hues Plus can now be bought
directly. Every Plus feature is also open to everyone for free from 30 October
to 1 November.

**13 August — 73 city pages.** Browse what's happening in a city before you get
there, with real local places for each one.

**11 August — Satellite view and a wider privacy circle.** Switch the map to
satellite imagery, and your privacy radius is now larger by default, so the area
you appear in is broader.

**7 August — Ads that don't track you.** Ads are matched to a general area only.
No profile of you is built, sold, or shared.

**6 August — Sign in with your username.** Your username works anywhere your
email did.

**4 August — Passkeys.** Sign in with Face ID or Touch ID — no password, no
email round trip. Sign-in links and password resets landed the same day.

**3 August — Easier on a phone.** The navigation collapses on small screens, so
more of the map is yours.

**2 August — A proper welcome.** The sign-in screen opens with a short intro.
Hold it to pause, tap to skip.

**1 August — 8hues.** New name, new home at 8hues.com, and a new logo. Read
receipts and incognito browsing arrived with it.

---

## July 2026

**29 July — Reviews, About me, and places.** Leave a review on a profile, write
a longer About me, and see the places people gather in your area.

**19 July — Travel mode.** Type any address and browse that area as if you were
there. Free, always.

---

## June 2026

**29 June — Who viewed me.** See who's been looking at your profile.

**20 June — Map filters.** Narrow the map to who's online, set how far out you
want to look, or search by name.

**19 June — Favourites, block and mute.** Star the people you want to find
again. Block anyone — it works both ways, immediately — or mute quietly.

**18 June — Notifications.** Get told about messages and activity even when the
app is closed.

**17 June — Video rooms.** One-to-one and group calls. Profile stats, badges
and interests arrived the same day.

**16 June — Photos, public chat, and a home-screen app.** Upload photos to your
profile, join the public chat, and install 8hues to your home screen.

**15 June — 8hues opens.** The live map, profiles, groups, location privacy and
the activity feed.
